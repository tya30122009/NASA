# Privacy Policy

QuickX is designed to protect your privacy.
- **No Accounts:** We do not collect any personal information or require accounts.
- **No Trackers:** We use no third-party cookies or tracking scripts.
- **Ephemeral Logs:** Access logs are kept for 7 days solely for abuse prevention and then deleted.
- **URL-based State:** Your search history and preferences stay in your browser's URL bar.
